Base on the findings in Q1, we can add relate soil pH with soil type and import them into regression. In Q1 figure suggests that the pattern between pH and height differs by soil type. Height rises clearly with pH in clay soil, but stays flat in chalky and sandy soils. Model 1 forces one common pH slope on all soils. Adding a pH by soil type would let the slope differ by soil and could fit better.

Also, height rises with sunlight and then flattens after about 6 hours, so extra sun helps less at high levels. A straight line misses this. Adding a squared sunlight term, or transforming sunlight, could capture the curve.
