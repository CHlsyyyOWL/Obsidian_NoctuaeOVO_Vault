There has been a controversy about what is the most effective way of teaching someone, especially in education. Some argues that the best way is to provide postive feedbacks to good behaviours and ignore the ill-behaved ones, on the mean time others may disagree as ignoring will not make mistakes naturally disappear. From my point of view, although commending your student does help in building a postive feedback loop, one can never simply ignore students' bad manner, as they can also learn from well-designed punishments.

Indeed compliments provide students' with enough confidence, as they receive more compliments from what they have done, they will establish a positive feedback loop in their mind. Such loop not only helps students behave better with great self-discipline in their future, but also makes following teaching works more efficient as students now trust their teacher more than before, deeply believing whatever practices assigned to them are good for their development.
