## 1. Introduction

【新写】本节 1.1 段。任务：用 institutional weakness 作为普世性开场，让非中国研究读者也能进入。参考写作：

Legal codification does not by itself guarantee that institutions constrain state action. Across Latin American constitutional rights delivery (Brinks, Levitsky and Murillo, 2019), the enforcement of urban regulations against street vending and land occupation in developing democracies (Holland, 2016, 2017), and the operation of formal financial regulation across emerging markets (Khanna and Palepu, 2010), a gap persists between what laws say and how institutions actually constrain decisions made under them. This gap has motivated a growing literature that treats institutional weakness not as a temporary imperfection but as a durable feature of many governance systems.

【新写】本节 1.2 段。任务：把 general framing 收缩到 Chinese specificity。参考写作：

China presents a distinctive form of this pattern. Rather than producing broad national statutes and confronting an enforcement gap, the Chinese state develops institutional design through ministerial orders, provincial decrees, and municipal experiments while formal national legislation is often delayed or absent (Heilmann, 2008; Tsai, Wang and Lin, 2021; Ma, 2024). The result is a governance architecture in which central authorities set direction through deliberately ambiguous framework documents and subnational governments produce heterogeneous responses that fill some spaces and leave others open (Ang, 2016; Zhou, 2017, 2022). Whether this architecture produces the same discretion-preserving outcomes as the more familiar Latin American or South Asian versions of institutional weakness, or whether it operates through mechanisms distinctive to Chinese central-local relations, has not been systematically examined in the credit governance domain.

The introduction of the credit repair mechanism marks a shift in China's corporate social credit system from punitive enforcement toward a more institutionalized rule-based compliance framework. The 2025 Administrative Measures for Credit Repair (NDRC Order No. 36, effective April 1, 2026) establish the first nationwide legal framework for restoring the credit standing of enterprises. This development follows a decade of system construction that proceeded in three broad phases. A foundation period from 2014 to 2020 was guided by the Planning Outline for the Construction of a Social Credit System. An expansion and enforcement period from 2020 to 2024 saw blacklisting reach massive scale. A regulatory refinement period from 2025 to 2026 began formalizing procedures for both punishment and repair.

The scale of the punitive phase created the conditions for reform. By 2019, Chinese courts had placed over 14.5 million entities on defaulting debtor lists, and travel restrictions had affected over 17 million individuals. Over 40 government bodies coordinated joint sanctions, meaning that a single blacklist entry could automatically trigger restrictions across multiple sectors. This mass punishment did not automatically produce behavioral correction but instead created a growing pool of economically blocked entities unable to participate in normal market activity. The 2020 Guiding Opinions on Further Improving the Social Credit System explicitly acknowledged the need for a credit repair mechanism, signaling a shift from viewing sanctions as a terminal state toward treating them as part of a regulatory cycle.

(改写) The 2025 Administrative Measures for Credit Repair create a unified national framework, **but they leave substantial discretion to provincial governments** on how to implement it. Provinces must decide how precisely to define "correcting the violation," what timelines to impose beyond the national minimums, how transparent to make the approval process, and how much operational discretion to retain for sectoral regulators. **Whether these choices vary systematically across provinces, and what drives the variation, are questions that existing scholarship has not addressed. The gap is partly a matter of timing. The 2025 Measures only came into effect on April 1, 2026, so provincial responses are still being formulated. Early comparative analysis can therefore capture institutional design choices before they become path-dependent.**

【新写】本节 1.4 段：central argument 段。任务：显式亮出术语链和中心论点。参考写作：

This paper argues that the 2025 to 2026 credit repair reform produces an institutional vacuum through a two-step mechanism. First, the central state engages in strategic under-specification, meaning it produces highly codified frameworks that deliberately leave substantive criteria for repair decisions undefined. Substantive criteria refers to the key judgment terms in the policy text ("已改正违法行为," "履行相应义务," "消除危害后果") that determine whether an applicant qualifies for repair. Second, provincial governments engage in selective non-response, meaning they respond to procedural and formal elements of the central framework but do not fill in the substantive criteria the central documents left open. Zhejiang produces an elaborate provincial statute that codifies procedures, timelines, and platforms without defining substantive criteria. Liaoning produces no provincial statute on credit repair while actively legislating in adjacent domains. Both responses converge on the same outcome, namely that the substantive judgment applied to any repair decision remains outside formal institutional constraint. The paper terms this outcome the substantive criteria vacuum, and identifies it as the specific institutional site through which discretion is preserved throughout the vertical governance chain.

【新写】本节 1.5 段：method and contribution。参考写作：

The paper applies the obligation-enforcement typology developed by Ma (2024) to three central baseline documents (State Council General Office Notice 2025 No. 22, NDRC Order No. 36, SAMR Order No. 107) plus the Zhejiang and Liaoning provincial responses. The contribution is threefold. First, the paper provides the first systematic comparison of provincial credit repair implementation under the 2025 framework. Second, it extends Ma's finding on household registration reform to market governance, showing that legalization without institutionalization is a pattern that operates across policy domains oriented in structurally different directions. Third, and most consequentially, it identifies the substantive criteria vacuum as the specific institutional site through which discretion is preserved, and shows that this site operates independently of whether provinces produce elaborate formal codification. The mechanism is falsifiable: if any subsequent provincial statute codifies substantive criteria for repair decisions with actionable specificity, the mechanism identified here would fail to describe the emerging institutional pattern.

### 2.1 Institutional Weakness in Comparative Perspective

第二段：introduce Holland's forbearance concept。Holland (2016, 2017) 提出 forbearance，定义为 "intentional and revocable nonenforcement of law"，即国家有能力执行但故意选择不执行。她的经验案例是 Bogotá 和 Santiago 的街头小贩执法与土地占领。用一句话说明她的关键 twist：不执法不是能力缺失而是政治选择。

第三段：说明本文与这些框架的关系。本文观察到的现象位于立法阶段而非执法阶段，因此不能完全归入 Holland 的 forbearance。本文与 Brinks 等人的 insignificance 有部分重叠——规则被制定但不覆盖核心内容——但差异在于中国的机制展现出跨行政层级的系统性协同，即中央和地方在各自层级都做同类留白，这不是单一决策者的 forbearance 而是纵向治理架构的 institutional feature。本文因此在既有 institutional weakness 议题里定位为一个新的 mechanism identification。

### 2.2 Chinese Central-Local Dynamics

(改写) The persistence of administrative discretion in Chinese governance despite formal codification is a well established observation in the scholarship on central-local relations. **Zhou (2017, 2022) identifies a fundamental tension between centralized authority and effective local administration across a vast, internally diverse territory. His concept of variable coupling holds that policy is made uniformly at the center while implementation is designed to be flexible. The center retains goal-setting and inspection authority while delegating operational discretion. Zhou Li-An (2014) develops the behavioral corollary through the concept of administrative subcontracting (行政发包制). The center packages policy tasks and delegates them to local governments, who gain implementation autonomy but bear the associated risks. Local officials therefore have a rational interest in preserving ambiguity rather than demanding precise central standards, because precise standards constrain their flexibility without reducing their accountability.**

【新写】本节 2.2 第二段。任务：引入 Heilmann 和 Ang 作为具体机制文献。参考写作：

Heilmann (2008) develops the framework of "experimentation under hierarchy" to describe how China produces policy change through local pilots that are subsequently generalized. Central authorities set the direction and monitor outcomes, while selected localities test institutional forms whose results feed back into national policy. Ang (2016) extends this line through the concept of "directed improvisation," arguing that Chinese subnational governments operate under central direction but exercise substantial improvisational autonomy, producing sub-national variation as a structural feature rather than a transitional condition. The two frameworks together give the vocabulary for describing what happens when provincial governments respond differently to the same national directive. Both frameworks predict variation but do not themselves specify what determines which parts of the central directive get responded to and which get left open. This paper identifies substantive criteria as the systematically un-responded portion, and this identification is the mechanism-level contribution the paper makes to the Heilmann-Ang line of scholarship.

【新写】本节 2.2 第三段。任务：引入 Ma (2024) 作为本文方法论的直接源头。参考写作：

Ma (2024) provides the most recent empirical application of these insights to a specific policy domain. Ma applies a two-dimensional obligation-enforcement typology to 492 household registration reform policy documents from 2011 to 2021, identifying a pattern in which formal hardening of policy at higher administrative ranks coexists with substantial administrative discretion at the implementation level. Ma's finding produces a working hypothesis for the present paper: the same pattern of top institutional design combined with discretion preservation may characterise the credit repair reform domain, but the specific site at which discretion is preserved requires empirical identification.

## 3. Theoretical Framework

### 3.1 Ma's Obligation-Enforcement Typology

Ma (2024) adapts the legalisation framework of Abbott, Keohane, Moravcsik, Slaughter and Snidal (2000) for the analysis of Chinese domestic policy. Ma reduces the original three dimensions of obligation, precision, and delegation into two operational dimensions of obligation and enforcement. Obligation measures the binding force of the policy on regulatory subjects, captured by indicators such as document issuer rank, target specificity, instrument specificity, and supplementary instruments. Enforcement measures the operational machinery for translating rules into decisions, captured by indicators such as inspection mechanisms, performance assessment, public disclosure, and coordination bodies. ### 3.2 Substantive Criteria: Definition and Location

【新写】本节 3.2 一段约 300 词。任务：显式定义 substantive criteria 是什么，为什么它是本文分析的核心。参考写作：

Substantive criteria refers to the specific judgment terms in a policy text that determine whether an applicant satisfies the conditions for a regulatory action. In the credit repair domain, these are the terms that determine whether an enterprise qualifies for having its blacklist entry removed. NDRC Order No. 36 requires the applicant to have "corrected the violation" (已改正违法行为), "fulfilled the relevant obligations" (履行相应义务), and made a "public credit pledge." SAMR Order No. 107 requires "active rectification" (主动纠正), "elimination of harm" (消除危害后果), and "absence of recurrence in the same category" (无同类违法行为再犯). None of these terms is defined in operational form in any of the three central documents. What counts as correction, what evidence establishes fulfillment, what standard measures the elimination of harm, and who bears the burden of proof are all questions the framework does not resolve.

The distinction between substantive criteria and other policy elements is essential to this paper's analysis. Table X below distinguishes the three classes of policy elements in the credit repair framework.

**Table X. Three Classes of Policy Elements in the Credit Repair Framework**

|Policy Element Class|Level of Codification|Examples|
|---|---|---|
|Procedural|Highly codified|Application timelines, review windows, platform entry points|
|Formal|Highly codified|Application material lists, dispute procedures, categorization schemes|
|**Substantive Criteria**|**Systematically left open**|**"Corrected the violation," "fulfilled obligations," "eliminated harm"**|

The empirical claim of this paper is that the third row remains open regardless of how heavily the first two rows are codified. Zhejiang produces a provincial decree with statutory force that codifies procedures and platforms in detail while leaving the third row open. Liaoning produces no provincial statute at all, which leaves all three rows to national defaults, but the national defaults themselves leave the third row open. The convergence at the third row is what this paper identifies as the substantive criteria vacuum.

### 3.3 The Terminology Chain: From Central Under-specification to Institutional Vacuum

【新写】本节 3.3 一段约 400 词。任务：把三个术语连起来讲清楚，形成本文的中心分析工具。参考写作：

This paper uses three linked terms to describe the mechanism through which discretion is preserved under the 2025 credit repair framework.

Strategic Under-specification refers to the central state's deliberate production of policy frameworks that codify procedural and formal elements heavily while leaving substantive criteria for regulatory decisions undefined. The under-specification is strategic in two senses. First, it operates at systematically decisive locations, namely the specific terms on which repair applications are judged rather than random gaps distributed across the text. Second, it persists across sequential drafting rounds of the same policy area, which distinguishes it from drafting lag or oversight.

Selective Non-Response refers to the pattern by which provincial governments respond to portions of the central directive while leaving other portions unresponded. Zhejiang's Decree No. 406 illustrates the pattern in one form: extensive provincial codification of procedures, platforms, and timelines combined with no provincial definition of substantive criteria. Liaoning illustrates the pattern in a different form: no provincial statute on credit repair combined with active provincial legislation in the adjacent Credit Certificate Substitution domain. Both forms converge on treating substantive criteria as the un-responded portion.

Institutional Vacuum refers to the outcome of central under-specification combined with provincial non-response at the same policy location. In an institutional vacuum, decisions are made on the ground and produce concrete outcomes for applicants, but the decision-making itself is not constrained by any codified rule at any administrative tier. The vacuum is not an accidental gap. It is the structural product of coordinated non-specification across the vertical governance chain.

The three terms describe a single mechanism at different points in the vertical chain. Strategic under-specification is the central input, selective non-response is the provincial translation, and institutional vacuum is the joint outcome. The mechanism is distinct from Holland's (2016) forbearance because it operates at the codification stage rather than the enforcement stage. It is distinct from Brinks, Levitsky and Murillo's (2019) insignificance because it produces active regulatory codification that simply omits the deciding elements, rather than producing rules that fail to affect behaviour. It shares Ang's (2016) directed improvisation framework at the descriptive level but identifies a specific site within that improvisation, namely substantive criteria, where the vertical coordination of non-specification systematically occurs.

### 3.4 Hypotheses

【新写】本节 3.4 一段约 250 词。任务：提出三个假说作为下一节 case analysis 的分析工具。参考写作：

The paper tests three hypotheses derived from the literature. H1 (Development Advantages) predicts that provinces with higher GDP per capita and more developed digital governance infrastructure produce provincial implementation with stronger obligation and stronger enforcement indicators, reflecting greater institutional capacity for procedural elaboration (Drinhausen and Brussee, 2021). H2 (Private Sector Concentration) predicts that provinces with higher private sector concentration produce repair terms more favorable to enterprises, reflecting structural demand from firms whose credit ratings affect access to export financing and procurement contracts. H3 (Political Influence Preservation) predicts that provinces where SCS implementation was most active during the 2020 to 2024 enforcement phase establish restoration mechanisms that preserve greater administrative discretion for sectoral regulators, consistent with administrative subcontracting logic under which local officials treat sanctioning and recovery powers as regulatory capital (Zhou, 2014; Creemers, 2018).

Underlying these three hypotheses is a central claim that this paper tests through the case analysis. The claim is that regardless of which hypothesis best explains procedural variation across provinces, the substantive criteria vacuum operates at all provinces observed. Selective non-response converges at the substantive criteria row across otherwise structurally different provinces. If this claim is correct, then H1 through H3 explain surface-level procedural divergence while the deeper mechanism operates uniformly.

## 5. Empirical Analysis

### 5.1 The National Baseline

The reform package consists of three central documents that any province must integrate. State Council General Office Notice 2025 No. 22, issued 22 June 2025, sets the macro framework. It specifies a ten-working-day overall timeline for credit repair processing, a three-working-day acceptance window, and a seven-working-day decision window. NDRC Order No. 36, issued 20 November 2025 and effective 1 April 2026, governs credit repair across all administrative domains. The order classifies misconduct as minor, general, or severe, with sectoral departments permitted to add categories of severe misconduct. Application conditions require correction of conduct, fulfilment of obligations, and a public credit pledge. The order establishes an administrative appeal channel through the Credit China website. SAMR Order No. 107, issued 21 November 2025 and effective 25 December 2025, governs credit repair within the market supervision domain only. The order uses the same trichotomy but quantifies monetary thresholds at 50,000 yuan for businesses and 5,000 yuan for individuals. Application conditions require active rectification, elimination of harm, and absence of recurrence in the same category. The order routes disputes to administrative review or court proceedings only.

**Table 1. Obligation and Enforcement Coding of the National Baseline**

|Indicator|SC Notice 22|NDRC Order 36|SAMR Order 107|
|---|---|---|---|
|Issuer rank|State Council General Office|National ministerial order|National ministerial order|
|Substantive criteria specificity|Macro timeline only|Three-tier classification with sectoral discretion on severe additions|Three-tier with quantified monetary thresholds and high-risk sector auto-routing|
|Application requirements|Not separately specified|Correction, fulfilment, public pledge|Active rectification, harm elimination, non-recurrence|
|Dispute resolution channel|Not specified|Administrative appeal via Credit China|Administrative review or court only|
|Platform infrastructure|No new platform|Credit China website|National platform integrated with NECIPS|

|Obligation rating|Moderate|Moderate|Strong|

(改写) The two ministerial orders differ in where substantive discretion is located. NDRC Order 36 delegates substantive discretion to sectoral departments by leaving phrases such as "corrected misconduct" and "other severe categories" open. SAMR Order 107 retains substantive discretion within the order itself through phrases such as "active rectification eliminating harm" and "same category violation." **The two orders differ in the location of preserved discretion but converge in preserving it. Neither order defines the terms on which repair decisions are actually made. This convergence at the substantive criteria row is the first observation supporting the vacuum hypothesis of Section 3.**

### 5.2 Zhejiang Implementation

Zhejiang issued Provincial Government Decree No. 406 in March 2025, six months before the publication of NDRC Order No. 36. The decree institutionalises credit repair at provincial level with statutory force, fixing jurisdictional boundaries, intake procedures, and decision timelines. The accompanying Credit Repair One Thing implementation scheme builds a unified intake platform on the Zhelibàn digital service infrastructure.

At municipal level, Hangzhou has layered two further mechanisms. The Three Documents Together practice attaches a Credit Repair Notification and a Compliance Recommendation Letter to every Administrative Penalty Decision. The Execution Observation Period mechanism, formalised in April 2026, allows officials to designate a temporary buffer between violation determination and penalty enforcement activation.

Zhejiang GDP per capita is approximately 141,000 yuan in 2025, ranking among the top five provinces nationally. Private sector employment exceeds 80 percent of total non-agricultural employment. Export-oriented enterprises constitute a substantial fraction of provincial corporate activity.

(改写) **Prior empirical evidence from Zhejiang provides additional context on how political considerations enter the credit governance infrastructure.** Lin and Milhaupt (2023) analysed corporate social credit scores for 531 A-share listed companies in Zhejiang, the only province to have published such scores publicly. **They found that politically connected firms received systematically higher scores regardless of ownership type or state equity share. The result was driven by a "social responsibility" category (18.5 percent of the total score) that awards points for government-issued honors, charitable donations to Party-sanctioned causes, and volunteer service in Party-recognized activities. Neither better-governed nor more profitable firms scored higher. This finding indicates that Zhejiang's advanced credit governance infrastructure already embeds political considerations within scoring outcomes, which is directly relevant to the substantive criteria vacuum discussed in Section 6.**

### 5.3 Liaoning Implementation

Liaoning has not issued any provincial statute on credit repair as of mid-2026. The provincial response has run through two distinct tracks. The Credit Certificate Substitution reform, jointly implemented in September 2025 by the Provincial Development and Reform Commission, the Public Security Department, and the Provincial Data Administration, replaces no-violation certifications with specialised credit reports. The platform had issued approximately 1,300 specialised reports replacing roughly 68,000 certifications by late 2025. The reform sits adjacent to credit repair rather than overlapping with it.

For credit repair narrowly, Liaoning's institutional activity is concentrated in two centre cities. Dalian's Free Trade Zone pilot, launched in May 2021, established 24-hour completion for enterprise credit repair as a first-in-the-country initiative. Shenyang's Credit Repair One Thing reform, introduced in November 2024, instituted immediate municipal-level approval and a dedicated guidance line. The Dalian Municipal Social Credit Ordinance of July 2021 provides a municipal statutory anchor for credit governance in general but does not codify credit repair criteria.

Liaoning GDP per capita is approximately 80,000 yuan in 2025. State-owned enterprises retain a dominant position in the provincial economy. Private sector activity is concentrated in Dalian and Shenyang.

### 5.4 Cross-Provincial Comparison

(改写) Figure 2 places the coded cases on the four-type matrix. Zhejiang's Decree No. 406 and SAMR Order 107 occupy Type 4 (strong obligation, strong enforcement). NDRC Order 36 and Hangzhou municipal instruments occupy Type 2 (weak to moderate obligation, strong enforcement). Liaoning provincial-level response occupies Type 1 to Type 2 borderline (weak obligation, weak to moderate enforcement).

At the provincial regulatory level, Zhejiang has codified credit repair through a provincial decree with statutory force, while Liaoning has not. Zhejiang's Decree No. 406 fixes timelines, intake procedures, and jurisdictional boundaries. Liaoning's provincial response has been concentrated in the adjacent Credit Certificate Substitution reform rather than in a credit repair statute.

At the platform infrastructure level, Zhejiang operates the Zhelibàn unified intake platform with tracking and feedback functions, while Liaoning relies on the national Credit China platform as the default interface for credit repair specifically. Liaoning's separate platform-building effort has been directed at the Credit Certificate Substitution infrastructure.

At the municipal innovation level, both provinces produce active local experimentation, but the form differs. Hangzhou's innovations (Three Documents Together, Execution Observation Period) extend the communicative scope and temporal structure of credit governance. Dalian and Shenyang's innovations (24-hour completion, immediate approval) compress administrative timelines within their territorial scope without altering substantive criteria.

(改写) **Three features of this comparison require analytical attention. First, both provincial profiles leave substantive criteria for repair decisions ("corrected violation," "fulfilled obligations") undefined at the provincial level. This is true in Zhejiang despite Decree No. 406's extensive procedural codification. It is also true in Liaoning despite the absence of any provincial statute. Second, the municipal innovations in both provinces either expand discretion (Hangzhou's Execution Observation Period adds a stage of official judgment) or compress processing time without touching substance (Dalian and Shenyang). Neither province's municipal experiments codify substantive criteria that the provincial or national framework left open. Third, three administrative tiers (national, provincial, municipal) all leave the same policy element open. This vertical convergence of non-specification, occurring at the same textual location across three tiers of governance, is the empirical foundation for the substantive criteria vacuum hypothesis developed in Section 6.**

## 6. Discussion

### 6.1 Primary Finding: The Substantive Criteria Vacuum

【新写】本节 6.1 约 500 词。任务：陈述本文的一级结论。这是全文最核心的段落，写作时要保持第三层收缩显性。参考写作结构：

第一段：restate the mechanism。要点：本文识别的核心机制是 substantive criteria vacuum，由中央的 strategic under-specification 与省级的 selective non-response 共同产生。substantive criteria vacuum 位于所有政策要素中的一个具体行——决定申请人能否获得修复的判断词。这一行在国家 (NDRC 36 与 SAMR 107)、省级 (Zhejiang Decree 406) 和市级 (Hangzhou 三书同达与执行观察期、Dalian 24 小时、Shenyang 即时审批) 三个层级都保持未定义。三个层级的留白不是随机分布而是系统性发生在同一位置。这一空间构成 institutional vacuum，即决策发生但没有任何成文规则约束决策依据。

第二段：hypothesis assessment 简明版。要点：三个假说的检验结果全部指向 substantive criteria vacuum 的稳健性。H1 部分成立，Zhejiang 的高发展水平使得省级立法早于国家框架六个月出台，但即使这一先行立法也没有定义 substantive criteria。四川异常点表明 H1 单独不足以解释省级变异。H2 与观察一致但缺 firm-level data 无法直接验证。H3 通过 Liaoning 的选择性立法模式获得间接支持。**核心论点是无论哪个假说更能解释 procedural divergence，substantive criteria vacuum 都在所有观察省份出现**。

第三段：mechanism 的可证伪性和范围。要点：本文识别的机制是可证伪的。如果任何后续省级立法把 substantive criteria 定义到 operational specificity，本文识别的机制就会 fail 描述新的制度模式。范围方面，本文分析限于 de jure 政策文本设计。decision practice 是否复制 de jure 模式是 Section 6.6 处理的独立问题。

### 6.2 Secondary Observation: Experimentation under Hierarchy

【新写】本节 6.2 约 350 词。任务：陈述二级观察一——试验田模式在两省案例中的体现。参考写作结构：

第一段：把 Zhejiang 的先行立法放在 Heilmann (2008) experimentation under hierarchy 框架里。Zhejiang Decree 406 早于 NDRC Order 36 六个月出台的时序是 experimentation under hierarchy 的典型体现。省级承担 institutional experimentation，中央保留 direction-setting 和 pattern-recognition 权力。Zhelibàn 数字平台是 institutional experimentation 的具体载体。用一句话说 Ang (2016) 的 directed improvisation 框架同样解释这一时序。

第二段：把 Dalian FTZ 2021 24 小时完成机制和 Shenyang 2024 即时审批放在同一框架里。municipal 层的 experimentation 是 hierarchy 下更下沉的一层。Dalian FTZ 作为国务院批准的自贸区，天然承担 institutional experimentation 功能。用一句话说这两个 municipal 试验点与 Zhejiang 的 provincial 试验之间的关系是并列而非替代——它们分别试验不同的 institutional dimension (Zhejiang 试验成文化程度、Dalian 试验时间压缩、Shenyang 试验审批集中)。

第三段：experimentation under hierarchy 与本文核心机制的关系。要点：experimentation 与 substantive criteria vacuum 并不冲突。experimentation 发生在程序、平台、时间等 codifiable 维度上。substantive criteria 从来不在 experimentation 的范围内。这个观察让本文的 primary finding 与 Heilmann-Ang 框架相容而非竞争——experimentation 解释 procedural 变异，substantive criteria vacuum 解释 non-experimentation 的一致模式。这两个 mechanism 在同一政策改革中并行运作。

### 6.3 Secondary Observation: Top-Level Design and Path Dependency

【新写】本节 6.3 约 350 词。任务：陈述二级观察二——顶层制度设计与路径依赖。参考写作结构：

第一段：Tsai, Wang, Lin (2021) 的 "top-level design and local discretion" 框架直接对应本文观察到的顶层设计模式。中央通过 NDRC 36 和 SAMR 107 两个部门规章设定框架方向而不通过全国人大立法，这一 institutional choice 本身就是 top-level design。全国人大层面至今没有《社会信用法》酝酿了将近十年但从未出台。这个"高层不通过立法"的选择是路径依赖的具体表现。

第二段：路径依赖具体运作。要点：中央选择通过 State Council Notice、Ministerial Orders 等规范性文件而非法律推进 SCS。这一选择在 SCS 领域延续了十二年 (2014 Planning Outline 到 2026 NDRC 36)。credit repair 是 SCS 领域首批把规范性文件提升到部门规章层级的动作，但仍然停在部门规章而非法律。这一层级选择本身在 Jiang (2020) 论证的中国 dang'an 制度传统里就有历史根源——档案制度从帝国时期到共产党执政一直通过行政规范而非法律运作。credit repair 通过部门规章推进的选择是这一传统的当代表现。

第三段：路径依赖与 substantive criteria vacuum 的关系。要点：路径依赖决定了 codification 的 institutional vehicle 是部门规章而非法律。部门规章天然给部委保留更大的定义弹性。国家人大立法通常需要更明确的实质定义，因为立法程序涉及多方协商和公众审议。选择部门规章而非法律，就是选择一个可以合法保留 substantive criteria 弹性的 institutional vehicle。**路径依赖因此不是 substantive criteria vacuum 的替代解释而是它的 institutional 前提条件**。

### 6.4 Secondary Observation: Widening Provincial Divergence

【新写】本节 6.4 约 250 词。任务：陈述二级观察三——省级响应差异带来的不平等加深。这一段要谨慎，因为你只有两省数据不足以做强 empirical claim。写作时保持推测语气。参考写作结构：

第一段：观察陈述。要点：Zhejiang 早于国家框架六个月出台省级立法，Liaoning 至 2026 年中仍无省级立法。这一时间差意味着 Zhejiang 企业享受成文化 credit repair 保护的时间比 Liaoning 企业早 15 个月以上。如果 credit repair 的成文化本身提供 institutional certainty 从而降低企业交易成本 (Fan and Chen, 2024 提供 SCS 促进 entrepreneurship 的证据)，那么 Zhejiang 在此期间获得的 institutional 优势会 compound 到 economic outcome 上。

第二段：谨慎语气与 scope。要点：本文数据不足以直接测量这一 compounding effect。但两省的省级响应差异模式与既有关于中国 regional inequality 的研究一致 (Ang, 2016)。**这一观察作为 next research 议题记录，而不是本文可以完全支撑的 finding**。四川异常点提醒我们省级响应速度不能简单归为 economic capacity 的函数，也有政治因素。因此简单的"经济发达省份响应快因此更加发达"链条需要考虑更多变量。

### 6.5 Robustness: Ruling Out Delay and Deference

(改写) **The core claim that substantive criteria vacuum reflects deliberate strategic under-specification requires ruling out two rival explanations. First, undefined criteria could reflect drafting lag, meaning provinces have not yet had time to specify criteria that they intend to specify eventually. Second, undefined criteria could reflect regulatory deference, meaning provinces intentionally leave criteria to national or ministerial documents that will supply the missing specification.**

The municipal-level evidence provides a robustness check against these alternatives. If undefined provincial criteria reflected drafting lag or regulatory deference, cities operationalizing repair applications should either supply the missing specification themselves or defer visibly to expected national specification. Neither pattern is observed. Hangzhou's Execution Observation Period adds a new stage of official discretion rather than defining criteria. Dalian's 24-hour completion mechanism and Shenyang's immediate approval mechanism compress processing time without altering substantive criteria. That the missing specification remains missing at three administrative tiers (national, provincial, municipal) despite active policy elaboration at each tier makes drafting lag and regulatory deference less plausible than deliberate preservation.

(改写) **The observational timeline further supports the deliberate preservation reading. By mid-2026 both the Zhejiang and national NDRC pathways have been operational for over a year. Liaoning's provincial legislative agenda shows no visible preparation for credit repair legislation. Meanwhile Liaoning has actively legislated in the adjacent Credit Certificate Substitution domain since September 2025, ruling out a general institutional passivity. Dalian's 2021 Municipal Social Credit Ordinance shows that legislative capacity for credit governance exists within Liaoning at the municipal level. Liaoning's provincial non-legislation on credit repair specifically is therefore better read as selective non-response rather than as institutional delay.**

### 6.6 Scope Statement

(改写) **This paper's contribution is limited to establishing the de jure baseline against which future de facto analysis can be conducted. Whether the substantive criteria vacuum identified in the codified texts also characterises actual repair decisions is a distinct empirical question. Decision-level data on approval rates, processing times in practice, and substantive criteria actually applied is not currently published by provincial platforms. If such data becomes available, the vacuum identified here could either be corroborated (comparable de facto discretion in both provinces despite different de jure vehicles) or complicated (systematically different de facto discretion despite the de jure similarity in leaving criteria undefined).**

The case selection covers two provinces and a small number of municipalities within each. Future expansion to additional provinces with different combinations of capacity, private sector concentration, and prior enforcement intensity would test whether the observed pattern generalises. Plausible additional cases include Guangdong, Sichuan, Jiangsu, and Heilongjiang, each of which represents a different combination of the structural conditions hypothesised to shape implementation. The reform package took effect between December 2025 and April 2026, so the window for institutional observation is short and provincial responses may continue to evolve. A longitudinal follow-up at six-month and twelve-month intervals would provide stronger evidence of stable institutional patterns.

## 7. Conclusion

【新写】Conclusion 约 400 词，三段结构。

第一段：重述一级结论。要点：本文证明在 2025 到 2026 credit repair 国家统一框架下，Zhejiang 和 Liaoning 产出了截然不同的省级 institutional response，但两者的 substantive criteria 都保持未定义。这一 vacuum 由中央的 strategic under-specification 与省级的 selective non-response 共同产生，位于所有政策要素中的一个具体行——决定申请人能否获得修复的判断词。三个行政层级 (国家、省级、市级) 在同一位置留白，说明这是纵向治理架构的 institutional feature 而不是任何单一层级的疏漏。

第二段：陈述 broader implication。要点：这一比较把 Ma (2024) 户籍改革发现的 legalization without institutionalization 扩展到市场治理。两个域的政策张力方向不同 (户籍中央希望松地方想收，credit repair 中央希望规范化避免合法性危机，地方想保留监管资本)，但两个域出现同一 mechanism。这说明该 mechanism 独立于任何单一政策域的政治经济结构，来自中国治理架构的更深层特征。本文识别的具体 institutional site (substantive criteria) 提供了检验这一 mechanism 在其他政策域是否运作的可 falsifiable 参考点。次级观察层面，本文补充了两省案例在 experimentation under hierarchy (Heilmann, 2008) 与 top-level design (Tsai, Wang, Lin, 2021) 框架中的具体表现，并为 provincial divergence 的加深趋势提供了初步观察。

第三段：回扣开篇的合法性危机。要点：credit repair 改革本身是对 2020-2024 惩戒阶段合法性危机的回应。formal path 已经建立，但那条 path 上的每个决定仍由 substantive criteria 未定义的空间中的具体官员做出。这一 institutional arrangement 究竟产生了改革意图建立的 corrective 功能，还是把 discretion 问题以 codified form 保留下来，是未来几年 de facto 运作会回答的问题。这个 open ending 保护论证边界，也承认 de jure/de facto 张力是下一步研究议题。
