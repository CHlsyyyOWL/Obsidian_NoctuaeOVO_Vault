## 1. Introduction 1/2

<font color="#ff0000">Legal codification does not by itself guarantee that institutions constrain state action. Across Latin American constitutional rights delivery (Brinks, Levitsky and Murillo, 2019), the enforcement of urban regulations against street vending and land occupation in developing democracies (Holland, 2016, 2017), and the operation of formal financial regulation across emerging markets (Khanna and Palepu, 2010), a gap persists between what laws say and how institutions actually constrain decisions made under them. This gap has motivated a growing literature that treats institutional weakness not as a temporary imperfection but as a durable feature of many governance systems. </font>这一段和problematique的重复太大了

**China presents a distinctive form of this pattern**. Rather than producing broad national statutes and confronting an enforcement gap, the Chinese state develops institutional design through ministerial orders, provincial decrees, and municipal experiments while formal national legislation is often delayed or absent (Heilmann, 2008; Tsai, Wang and Lin, 2021; Ma, 2024). The result is a governance architecture in which central authorities set direction through deliberately ambiguous framework documents and subnational governments produce heterogeneous responses that fill some spaces and leave others open (Ang, 2016; Zhou, 2017, 2022). Whether this architecture produces the same discretion-preserving outcomes as the more familiar Latin American or South Asian versions of institutional weakness, or whether it operates through mechanisms distinctive to Chinese central-local relations, has not been systematically examined in the credit governance domain.

<font color="#ff0000">The 2025 to 2026 corporate credit repair reform </font>（不准确）provides a well-suited observation window for examining this question. Introduced through the Administrative Measures for Credit Repair (NDRC Order No. 36, effective April 1, 2026) and its market-supervision counterpart (SAMR Order No. 107), the reform establishes the first nationwide legal framework for restoring the credit standing of enterprises previously placed on defaulting debtor lists. Section 2 develops the historical and institutional background of this reform. The remainder of the Introduction states the central argument, method, and contribution.

This paper argues that the 2025 to 2026 credit repair reform produces an institutional vacuum through a two-step mechanism. First, the central state engages in strategic under-specification, meaning it produces highly codified frameworks that deliberately leave substantive criteria for repair decisions undefined. Substantive criteria refers to the key judgment terms in the policy text ("已改正违法行为," "履行相应义务," "消除危害后果") that determine whether an applicant qualifies for repair. Second, provincial governments engage in selective non-response, meaning they respond to procedural and formal elements of the central framework but do not fill in the substantive criteria the central documents left open. Zhejiang produces an elaborate provincial statute that codifies procedures, timelines, and platforms without defining substantive criteria. Liaoning produces no provincial statute on credit repair while actively legislating in adjacent domains. Both responses converge on the same outcome, namely that the substantive judgment applied to any repair decision remains outside formal institutional constraint. The paper terms this outcome the substantive criteria vacuum, and identifies it as the specific institutional site through which discretion is preserved throughout the vertical governance chain.

The paper applies the obligation-enforcement typology developed by Ma (2024) to three central baseline documents (State Council General Office Notice 2025 No. 22, NDRC Order No. 36, SAMR Order No. 107) plus the Zhejiang and Liaoning provincial responses. The contribution is threefold. First, the paper provides the first systematic comparison of provincial credit repair implementation under the 2025 framework. Second, it extends Ma's finding on household registration reform to market governance, showing that legalization without institutionalization is a pattern that operates across policy domains oriented in structurally different directions. Third, and most consequentially, it identifies the substantive criteria vacuum as the specific institutional site through which discretion is preserved, and shows that this site operates independently of whether provinces produce elaborate formal codification. The mechanism is falsifiable. If any subsequent provincial statute codifies substantive criteria for repair decisions with actionable specificity, the mechanism identified here would fail to describe the emerging institutional pattern.

## 3. Problematique 1/2 

Institutionalization does not follow automatically from legalization. Across cases as different as Latin American constitutional rights (Brinks, Levitsky and Murillo, 2019), Indian informal welfare regimes (Holland, 2017), and post-communist transition economies (Khanna and Palepu, 2010), formal legal frameworks routinely fail to produce the institutional constraint their text nominally implies. The gap between legalization and institutionalization appears in three recurring contexts. First, in emerging economies where legal capacity has developed faster than administrative implementation capacity. Second, in political systems where central legislation is used strategically to signal reform commitments without disturbing existing distributional arrangements. Third, in governance architectures where central and local authorities operate under different incentive structures and produce sequentially non-aligned responses to the same legal text.

China occupies a distinctive position within this comparative landscape. Institutional codification proceeds primarily through ministerial orders and provincial decrees rather than through National People's Congress legislation. National-level laws on major policy areas including social credit have been drafted and re-drafted for over a decade without passage. This is not a matter of legislative capacity, since the NPC produces substantial legislation in other domains. It reflects an institutional choice to conduct policy development through sub-legislative instruments that preserve interpretive flexibility across administrative tiers. Whether this choice systematically produces discretion-preserving outcomes at the level of concrete regulatory decisions, and whether the mechanism converges on a specific institutional site across otherwise different provincial responses, is a question that existing scholarship on Chinese governance has not directly addressed.

This paper takes the 2025 to 2026 corporate credit repair reform as an observation window through which to examine this question. The reform is well suited to the purpose for three reasons. First, it is recent enough that provincial responses can be observed as they form rather than as path-dependent equilibria. Second, it involves both a national framework and structurally different provincial responses, allowing within-China comparison under a constant national baseline. Third, it operates in market governance rather than social policy, providing a different policy domain from Ma's (2024) household registration analysis and thereby testing whether the discretion-preserving mechanism operates across policy domains with opposing central-local incentive alignments. The specific research question is whether provincial implementation differences under the 2025 framework converge on an identifiable institutional site at which formal codification and effective institutional constraint diverge.

### 4.2 Chinese Central-Local Dynamics 1/2

<font color="#ff0000">The pattern （of centralized directives combined with heavily localized implementation) is not a recent development in Chinese governance. 重点前置，加一句强调为什么我们要解释这个央地分离的传统，因为这个有助于我们理解这个问题——怎么有助于我们理解这个问题？</font>

In the late imperial period, county magistrates received broad instructions from higher levels and worked through local gentry to translate them into concrete action, producing what Fei Xiaotong (1948) described as a granular society in which state authority reached the sub-county level only through negotiated intermediation. The pattern was reproduced in the early reform era through what Chinese officials themselves termed "crossing the river by feeling for stones" (摸着石头过河), an incrementalist approach in which policy directions were set centrally while implementation modes were left to local experimentation. This tradition frames the more systematic contemporary mode of central-local coordination reviewed below.

Since the 1990s, this incrementalism has evolved into a more systematic mode of policymaking with three institutional components. Central leadership sets broad direction through top-level design (顶层设计), framework documents that specify goals without specifying operational content. Selected localities carry out pilot programs (试点) that test institutional forms, with successful pilots eligible for national generalization. Central leading small groups (中央领导小组) coordinate cross-departmental oversight without producing binding rules. Heilmann (2008) identifies this pattern as "experimentation under hierarchy," documenting how central authorities set the direction and monitor outcomes while selected localities test institutional forms whose results feed back into national policy. Ang (2016) extends this line through the concept of "directed improvisation," arguing that Chinese subnational governments operate under central direction while exercising substantial improvisational autonomy, producing sub-national variation as a structural feature rather than a transitional condition. Tsai, Wang and Lin (2021) document this pattern in the SCS domain specifically. Their fieldwork with mainland officials shows how local implementers had to interpret deliberately vague central directives and selectively implement provisions according to political risk. Business-facing credit regulation advanced most rapidly because it carried the lowest political risk, while citizen-facing credit scoring remained at a preliminary stage. The vagueness of the 2014 Planning Outline was not a drafting failure but a functional feature enabling this selective implementation.

The behavioral logic underlying this pattern has been theorized in two adjacent frameworks. Zhou Xueguang (2017, 2022) identifies a fundamental tension between centralized authority and effective local administration across a vast, internally diverse territory. His concept of variable coupling holds that policy is made uniformly at the center while implementation is designed to be flexible, with the center retaining goal-setting and inspection authority while delegating operational discretion. Zhou Li-An (2014) develops the behavioral corollary through the concept of administrative subcontracting (行政发包制). The center packages policy tasks and delegates them to local governments, who gain implementation autonomy but bear the associated risks. Local officials therefore have a rational interest in preserving ambiguity rather than demanding precise central standards, because precise standards constrain their flexibility without reducing their accountability. This tradition has both enabling and constraining implications for the credit repair reform analyzed in this paper. The enabling side is well documented, since the mode has produced substantial policy learning and allowed rapid adaptation across highly diverse local conditions. The constraining side is less examined. When the mode is applied to institutional codification rather than economic experimentation, the central preference for framework language and the local preference for interpretive discretion converge on producing text that is procedurally elaborate but substantively open. Ma (2024) provides the most recent empirical demonstration of this convergence through analysis of 492 household registration reform documents from 2011 to 2021, identifying a pattern in which formal hardening at higher administrative ranks coexists with substantial discretion at implementation. Ma's finding produces the working hypothesis for the present paper.

 Ma (2024) adapts the legalisation framework of Abbott, Keohane, Moravcsik, Slaughter and Snidal (2000) for the analysis of Chinese domestic policy. Ma reduces the original three dimensions of obligation, precision, and delegation into two operational dimensions of obligation and enforcement. Obligation measures the binding force of the policy on regulatory subjects. It captures whether the policy legally binds the actors it addresses and how tightly. Ma operationalizes obligation through indicators such as document issuer rank, target specificity, instrument specificity, and supplementary instruments. Enforcement measures the operational machinery for translating rules into decisions. It captures whether the policy contains the institutional infrastructure that would allow the rules to be applied in specific cases. Ma operationalizes enforcement through indicators such as inspection mechanisms, performance assessment, public disclosure, and coordination bodies. The two dimensions yield a four-cell matrix. Type 1 combines weak obligation with weak enforcement. Type 2 combines weak obligation with strong enforcement. Type 3 combines strong obligation with weak enforcement. Type 4 combines strong obligation with strong enforcement. Ma's empirical application to household registration reform finds that formal hardening at higher administrative ranks coexists with substantial administrative discretion at the implementation level, producing a pattern this paper terms legalization without institutionalization. The working hypothesis for the credit repair domain is that the same pattern may operate but at a specific institutional site that requires empirical identification.

In the credit repair domain, these are the terms that determine whether an enterprise qualifies for having its blacklist entry removed. NDRC Order No. 36 requires the applicant to have "corrected the violation" (已改正违法行为), "fulfilled the relevant obligations" (履行相应义务), and made a "public credit pledge." SAMR Order No. 107 requires "active rectification" (主动纠正), "elimination of harm" (消除危害后果), and "absence of recurrence in the same category" (无同类违法行为再犯). None of these terms is defined in operational form in any of the three central documents. What counts as correction, what evidence establishes fulfillment, what standard measures the elimination of harm, and who bears the burden of proof are all questions the framework does not resolve. The distinction between substantive criteria and other policy elements is essential to this paper's analysis. Table 1 distinguishes the three classes of policy elements in the credit repair framework.

**Table 1. Three Classes of Policy Elements in the Credit Repair Framework**

|Policy Element Class|Level of Codification|Examples|
|---|---|---|
|Procedural|Highly codified|Application timelines, review windows, platform entry points|
|Formal|Highly codified|Application material lists, dispute procedures, categorization schemes|

The empirical claim of this paper is that the third row remains open regardless of how heavily the first two rows are codified. Zhejiang produces a provincial decree with statutory force that codifies procedures and platforms in detail while leaving the third row open. Liaoning produces no provincial statute at all, which leaves all three rows to national defaults, but the national defaults themselves leave the third row open. The convergence at the third row is what this paper identifies as the substantive criteria vacuum.

This paper uses three linked terms to describe the mechanism through which discretion is preserved under the 2025 credit repair framework. Strategic Under-specification refers to the central state's deliberate production of policy frameworks that codify procedural and formal elements heavily while leaving substantive criteria for regulatory decisions undefined. The under-specification is strategic in two senses. First, it operates at systematically decisive locations, namely the specific terms on which repair applications are judged rather than random gaps distributed across the text. Second, it persists across sequential drafting rounds of the same policy area, which distinguishes it from drafting lag or oversight. Selective Non-Response refers to the pattern by which provincial governments respond to portions of the central directive while leaving other portions unresponded. Zhejiang's Decree No. 406 illustrates the pattern in one form, showing extensive provincial codification of procedures, platforms, and timelines combined with no provincial definition of substantive criteria. Liaoning illustrates the pattern in a different form, showing no provincial statute on credit repair combined with active provincial legislation in the adjacent Credit Certificate Substitution domain. Both forms converge on treating substantive criteria as the un-responded portion. Institutional Vacuum refers to the outcome of central under-specification combined with provincial non-response at the same policy location. In an institutional vacuum, decisions are made on the ground and produce concrete outcomes for applicants, but the decision-making itself is not constrained by any codified rule at any administrative tier. The vacuum is not an accidental gap. It is the structural product of coordinated non-specification across the vertical governance chain. The three terms describe a single mechanism at different points in the vertical chain. Strategic under-specification is the central input, selective non-response is the provincial translation, and institutional vacuum is the joint outcome. The mechanism is distinct from Holland's (2016) forbearance because it operates at the codification stage rather than the enforcement stage. It is distinct from Brinks, Levitsky and Murillo's (2019) insignificance because it produces active regulatory codification that simply omits the deciding elements, rather than producing rules that fail to affect behaviour. It shares Ang's (2016) directed improvisation framework at the descriptive level but identifies a specific site within that improvisation, namely substantive criteria, where the vertical coordination of non-specification systematically occurs.

The paper tests three hypotheses derived from the literature. H1 (Development Advantages) predicts that provinces with higher GDP per capita and more developed digital governance infrastructure produce provincial implementation with stronger obligation and stronger enforcement indicators, reflecting greater institutional capacity for procedural elaboration (Drinhausen and Brussee, 2021). H2 (Private Sector Concentration) predicts that provinces with higher private sector concentration produce repair terms more favorable to enterprises, reflecting structural demand from firms whose credit ratings affect access to export financing and procurement contracts. H3 (Political Influence Preservation) predicts that provinces where SCS implementation was most active during the 2020 to 2024 enforcement phase establish restoration mechanisms that preserve greater administrative discretion for sectoral regulators, consistent with administrative subcontracting logic under which local officials treat sanctioning and recovery powers as regulatory capital (Zhou, 2014; Creemers, 2018). Underlying these three hypotheses is a central claim that this paper tests through the case analysis. The claim is that regardless of which hypothesis best explains procedural variation across provinces, the substantive criteria vacuum operates at all provinces observed. Selective non-response converges at the substantive criteria row across otherwise structurally different provinces. If this claim is correct, then H1 through H3 explain surface-level procedural divergence while the deeper mechanism operates uniformly.

## 6. Methodology  ❌

The paper codes each policy document on six indicators. Issuer rank captures the administrative level of the issuing authority, ranging from municipal work outlines at the low end to National People's Congress legislation at the high end. Target specificity captures whether the policy addresses a defined regulatory subject with specific attributes or a general class of actors. Instrument specificity captures whether the policy prescribes concrete regulatory instruments or leaves instrument choice to implementing authorities. Substantive criteria specificity captures whether the policy defines the terms on which regulatory decisions are actually made, or leaves those terms to the discretion of decision-making officials. Platform infrastructure captures whether the policy establishes or requires a specific operational platform for processing regulatory actions. Coordination scope captures whether the policy establishes cross-departmental coordination requirements or operates within a single department. Each document receives an obligation rating and an enforcement rating on a three-point scale of Weak, Moderate, and Strong. Obligation is rated Strong when the document combines high issuer rank with specific targets and mandatory instrument language. Obligation is rated Weak when the document combines low issuer rank with vague targets and hortatory instrument language. Enforcement is rated Strong when the document establishes operational platforms, inspection mechanisms, and cross-departmental coordination. Enforcement is rated Weak when none of these operational infrastructures is established. Moderate ratings apply when the document contains partial elements of the corresponding Strong rating. The two ratings jointly place the document in one of Ma's four cells. The coding unit is the document, but each province's multiple documents combined give the provincial institutional profile. The substantive criteria specificity indicator receives particular analytical weight because this indicator is the one at which the paper predicts systematic convergence across cases.

The paper uses Zhejiang and Liaoning as a most-different systems comparison on the three hypotheses. MERICS (2021, updated 2022) mapping data on SCS-related document output across provinces shows dramatic regional variation, with economically developed coastal provinces producing more than 40 policy documents and several northeastern and western provinces producing fewer than 10 during the 2003 to 2020 window. Zhejiang and Liaoning occupy opposite positions in this distribution. On H1, Zhejiang's GDP per capita of approximately 141,000 yuan in 2025 is roughly twice Liaoning's 80,000 yuan, and Zhejiang's digital economy accounts for 35 percent of total provincial output compared to 24 percent for Liaoning. On H2, Zhejiang's private sector accounts for over 80 percent of non-agricultural employment while Liaoning retains a dominant state-owned enterprise structure. On H3, Zhejiang produced more than 40 SCS-related documents during 2003 to 2020 while Liaoning produced fewer than 10. Despite these structural differences, both provinces face the same national framework established by NDRC Order No. 36, SAMR Order No. 107, and State Council General Office Notice 2025 No. 22. The comparison therefore isolates the effect of provincial structural conditions on implementation choices while holding the national framework constant. A third province, Sichuan, is not coded in this paper but is referenced in Section 8 as an anomaly for H1. Sichuan's GDP per capita in 2024 was approximately 82,000 yuan, comparable to Liaoning's 80,000 yuan, yet Sichuan's SCS policy document output places it in a higher tier than several wealthier eastern provinces. The Sichuan anomaly is used analytically without full coding to argue that H1 alone cannot account for provincial variation.：

The analysis relies on publicly available policy documents at three administrative tiers (national, provincial, municipal), on official platform documentation, and on secondary reporting. Decision-level data on repair approvals, processing times in practice, and substantive criteria applied in specific cases is not currently published by provincial platforms. The scope of this paper is therefore limited to de jure policy design rather than decision practice. Whether the substantive criteria vacuum identified in the codified texts also characterises actual repair decisions is a distinct empirical question addressed in Section 8. This scope constraint is not a limitation to be minimized but a boundary that determines what the paper can and cannot claim. Findings about de jure institutional design cannot be directly extrapolated to de facto discretion patterns without additional evidence. The paper's contribution is to establish the de jure baseline against which future de facto analysis can be conducted.

【新写】本节 8.2 约 350 词。任务：陈述二级观察一——试验田模式在两省案例中的体现。参考写作结构：

### 8.3 Top-Level Design and Bypassing National Legislation (Secondary Observation)

【新写】本节 8.3 约 500 词。任务：陈述二级观察二——顶层设计与绕开全国立法。参考写作结构：

第一段：Tsai, Wang, Lin (2021) 的 "top-level design and local discretion" 框架直接对应本文观察到的顶层设计模式。中央通过 NDRC 36 和 SAMR 107 两个部门规章设定框架方向而不通过全国人大立法，这一 institutional choice 本身就是 top-level design。全国人大层面至今没有《社会信用法》酝酿了将近十年但从未出台。这个"高层不通过立法"的选择是路径依赖的具体表现。

第二段：路径依赖具体运作。要点：中央选择通过 State Council Notice、Ministerial Orders 等规范性文件而非法律推进 SCS。这一选择在 SCS 领域延续了十二年 (2014 Planning Outline 到 2026 NDRC 36)。credit repair 是 SCS 领域首批把规范性文件提升到部门规章层级的动作，但仍然停在部门规章而非法律。这一层级选择本身在 Jiang (2020) 论证的中国 dang'an 制度传统里就有历史根源——档案制度从帝国时期到共产党执政一直通过行政规范而非法律运作。credit repair 通过部门规章推进的选择是这一传统的当代表现。

第三段：通过地方法规绕开国家立法的机制。要点：**这一段留位置给你补摩托车上高速的类比或找一个替代案例（比如烟花爆竹禁放的国家规定与地方执行差异、共享单车管理的国家指导与地方细则冲突）**。这一段的论点是：国家层级刻意保留模糊性，让地方规定在具体操作中承担实质规则的功能。这不是国家能力缺失，而是把 regulatory space 主动让渡给地方作为治理工具。credit repair 恰好是这一机制的当代具体表现——国家部门规章保留 substantive criteria 模糊，省级立法可以填也可以不填，都在国家框架允许的范围内。

第四段：路径依赖与 substantive criteria vacuum 的关系。要点：路径依赖决定了 codification 的 institutional vehicle 是部门规章而非法律。部门规章天然给部委保留更大的定义弹性。国家人大立法通常需要更明确的实质定义，因为立法程序涉及多方协商和公众审议。选择部门规章而非法律，就是选择一个可以合法保留 substantive criteria 弹性的 institutional vehicle。**路径依赖因此不是 substantive criteria vacuum 的替代解释而是它的 institutional 前提条件**。

### 8.4 Widening Provincial Divergence (Secondary Observation)

【新写】本节 8.4 约 400 词。任务：陈述二级观察三——省级响应差异带来的不平等加深。参考写作结构：

第一段：观察陈述。要点：Zhejiang 早于国家框架六个月出台省级立法，Liaoning 至 2026 年中仍无省级立法。这一时间差意味着 Zhejiang 企业享受成文化 credit repair 保护的时间比 Liaoning 企业早 15 个月以上。Zhejiang 因此获得 institutional 优势——企业交易成本降低、修复申请预期稳定、跨省投资信心增强。这些优势通过 credit repair 的成文化实现，不需要额外制度投入。

第二段：causal chain 陈述。要点：如果这一 institutional 优势与省级经济表现正相关，那么响应快的省份获得双重收益——政策工具优势加经济基础优势。响应慢的省份则相反——既没有政策工具支持又缺乏经济发展基础。这条 causal chain 的具体运作是：部分省份先进 → 政策铺开时间不均 → 地方响应差异 → 差异深化 → 经济好的省份响应更快因此更好、经济差的省份响应更慢因此更差。这一循环加深省级发展不平等。Ang (2016) 的 sub-national variation 研究提供了这一类循环在中国治理中的既有观察。

第三段：Sichuan 异常点作为反证。要点：四川的存在复杂化上面这条因果链。四川经济基础与辽宁相近但 SCS 参与度接近沿海顶层。这说明省级响应速度不能简单归为经济能力的函数，也有政治因素——包括省级领导对 credit governance 作为地方 regulatory capital 的重视程度、省级党委对 SCS 政策的立场、地方与中央部委的关系密度。因此"经济发达省份响应快因此更加发达"的直线因果需要考虑更多变量。

第四段：scope 声明。要点：本文数据不足以直接测量 provincial divergence 与经济结果的 compounding effect。**这一观察作为 next research 议题记录，而不是本文可以完全支撑的 finding**。但两省的观察模式与既有关于中国 regional inequality 的研究方向一致，值得作为 secondary observation 记录，为后续研究提供假说。

## 9. Conclusion

【新写】Conclusion 约 500 词，三段结构。

第一段：三个 pattern 一起构成中国政策实施的惯性模式。要点：本文识别了三个 pattern 共同构成中国政策实施的惯性模式。第一是顶层设计通过部门规章而非全国立法完成，把 institutional design 权保留在中央部委手中。第二是试验田模式，选定地方承担 institutional experimentation，成功的实验向上传递为国家模式。第三是 substantive criteria vacuum，中央和地方在实质判断标准上共同留白，为具体决策官员保留裁量空间。三个 pattern 中，substantive criteria vacuum 是最具体、最可 falsifiable 的一个，因为它可以通过具体政策文本的编码直接观察和验证。其他两个 pattern 更接近 governance style 层次，需要更长时段和更多案例才能确证。本文的经验贡献主要落在 substantive criteria vacuum 这一具体机制的识别。

第二段：这些 pattern 产生的政策效应。要点：这三个 pattern 组合产生几个可观察的政策效应。见效周期长——政策工具通过部门规章而非法律推进，且依赖地方响应速度，铺开需要时间。地方响应不均——省级政府根据自身结构条件和政治判断选择响应速度和响应形式。地区不平等深化——响应快的地方获得政策工具和经济结构的双重优势，响应慢的地方陷入相反循环。制度真空长期存在——substantive criteria 的策略性留白在国家、省、市三个层级都保持，因此这一 vacuum 不会因为某一层级的新立法自动关闭。这些效应对本文分析的 credit repair 改革意味着：改革的形式路径已经建立，但实质保护是否到位取决于具体地方和具体官员的判断，而这些判断不受任何统一成文规则约束。

第三段：回扣开篇的合法性危机。要点：credit repair 改革本身是对 2020 到 2024 惩戒阶段合法性危机的回应。惩戒阶段的合法性问题是 14.5M+ 主体面临制裁而没有明确恢复路径。改革建立了 formal path，但那条 path 上的每个决定仍由 substantive criteria 未定义的空间中的具体官员做出。改革究竟产生了它意图建立的 corrective 功能，还是把 discretion 问题以 codified form 保留下来，是未来几年 de facto 运作会回答的问题。本文的贡献不是解答这一问题，而是识别出问题会以何种具体形式在制度架构中出现——即通过 substantive criteria vacuum 这一具体 institutional site 的运作方式。这一识别为后续 de facto 分析提供了明确的观察焦点。

## 写作 checklist

标点：全文不允许 semicolon (；), colon 除非在 references 和引文中 (:), em dash (—), 用作破折号的 hyphen (-)。

段首句：每段开头一句话是这段的中心句，不用"One may ask"、"It is worth noting"、"Let me explain"。

连接词：段间用 however、therefore、because、although、given that 等常见连接词，不用 nonetheless 之外的低频词。

词汇：避免 multifaceted, delve, nuanced, underscore, pivotal, facilitate, leverage (as verb), robust (outside statistical usage), holistic, paradigm。

论证顺序：先给结论，再给证据。段落里 claim → data → interpretation 的顺序。

**术语一致性**：全文使用 Strategic Under-specification / Selective Non-Response / Institutional Vacuum / Substantive Criteria 四个术语。第一次出现时都在 Section 5 定义清楚，之后各处引用都指向 Section 5 定义。**不允许在其他章节临时替换为同义表述**。

**收缩层次显性**：核心结论句必须落在第三层或第四层。特别检查 Section 1.4、Section 5 第三段、Section 8.1、Section 9，这四处最容易滑回第一层的"中国治理保留裁量"宏观表述。

**Case selection**：Section 6 第二段必须显式说明 most-different systems design 与三个 H 的映射。

**Substantive Criteria 定义**：Section 5 第二段必须列出所有具体判断词并说明这些词在哪些文件里出现且都未被定义。

**Intentionality**：Section 8.1 第三段必须处理 drafting lag、regulatory deference、观望假说三个 rival explanations。

**Scope**：Section 6 第三段必须写清楚 de jure vs de facto 分界。

**摩托车类比或替代案例**：Section 8.3 第三段留了位置给你补一个国家法与地方规冲突的具体案例。使用前必须自己核实法律条文和地方规定。

**Sofia 交稿后的整合**：Sofia 交 Section 2 Background 之后你需要做三件事。第一，标点纪律清理（去掉所有 semicolon、em dash、正文里的 colon）。第二，检查 Section 3.3 SCS Scholarship 里 Jiang 引用没有重复 Section 2 的历史事实。第三，检查 Section 1.3 转场句"Section 2 develops the historical and institutional background"是否与 Sofia 实际写出的 Section 2 内容匹配，如不匹配调整转场句。

**投稿前 references 核实**：Chen, Loefflad, Grossklags (2026) 已核实。Tsai, Wang, Lin (2021) 已核实。所有其他引用作者名、期刊卷号、页码 draft 完成后再统一核对一次。
