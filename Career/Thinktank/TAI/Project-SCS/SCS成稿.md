## 1. Introduction

The introduction of the credit repair mechanism marks a shift in China's corporate social credit system from punitive enforcement toward a more institutionalized rule-based compliance framework. The 2025 Administrative Measures for Credit Repair (NDRC Order No. 36, effective April 1, 2026) establish the first nationwide legal framework for restoring the credit standing of enterprises. This development follows a decade of system construction that proceeded in three broad phases. A foundation period from 2014 to 2020 was guided by the Planning Outline for the Construction of a Social Credit System. An expansion and enforcement period from 2020 to 2024 saw blacklisting reach massive scale. A regulatory refinement period from 2025 to 2026 began formalizing procedures for both punishment and repair.

The scale of the punitive phase created the conditions for reform. By 2019, Chinese courts had placed over 14.5 million entities on defaulting debtor lists, and travel restrictions had affected over 17 million individuals. Over 40 government bodies coordinated joint sanctions, meaning that a single blacklist entry could automatically trigger restrictions across multiple sectors. This mass punishment did not automatically produce behavioral correction but instead created a growing pool of economically blocked entities unable to participate in normal market activity. The 2020 Guiding Opinions on Further Improving the Social Credit System explicitly acknowledged the need for a credit repair mechanism, signaling a shift from viewing sanctions as a terminal state toward treating them as part of a regulatory cycle.

(改写) The 2025 Administrative Measures for Credit Repair create a unified national framework, **but they leave substantial discretion to provincial governments** on how to implement it. Provinces must decide how precisely to define "correcting the violation," what timelines to impose beyond the national minimums, how transparent to make the approval process, and how much operational discretion to retain for sectoral regulators. **Whether these choices vary systematically across provinces, and what drives the variation, are questions that existing scholarship has not addressed. The gap is partly a matter of timing. The 2025 Measures only came into effect on April 1, 2026, so provincial responses are still being formulated. Early comparative analysis can therefore capture institutional design choices before they become path-dependent.**

### 2.2 Discretion Preservation in Chinese Governance

(改写) The persistence of administrative discretion in Chinese governance despite formal codification is a well established observation in the scholarship on central-local relations. **Zhou (2017, 2022) identifies a fundamental tension between centralized authority and effective local administration across a vast, internally diverse territory. His concept of variable coupling holds that policy is made uniformly at the center while implementation is designed to be flexible. The center retains goal-setting and inspection authority while delegating operational discretion. Zhou Li-An (2014) develops the behavioral corollary through the concept of administrative subcontracting (行政发包制). The center packages policy tasks and delegates them to local governments, who gain implementation autonomy but bear the associated risks. Local officials therefore have a rational interest in preserving ambiguity rather than demanding precise central standards, because precise standards constrain their flexibility without reducing their accountability.**

Ma (2024) provides the most recent empirical application of this insight to a specific policy domain. Ma applies a two-dimensional obligation-enforcement typology to 492 household registration reform policy documents from 2011 to 2021, identifying a pattern in which formal hardening of policy at higher administrative ranks coexists with substantial administrative discretion at the implementation level. This produces a working hypothesis for the present paper, namely that the same pattern of top institutional design combined with discretion preservation may also characterise the credit repair reform domain.

(改写) Tsai, Wang and Lin (2021) provide complementary evidence from a different angle. **Their fieldwork in an anonymized Chinese city on social credit implementation found that the deliberately vague 2014 Planning Outline forced local officials to interpret the center's implicit signals and selectively implement provisions according to political risk. Business-facing credit regulation advanced most rapidly because it carried the lowest political risk, while citizen-facing credit scoring remained at a preliminary stage. This asymmetry supports the interpretation that ambiguity in central frameworks is not a drafting failure but a functional feature of the governance architecture.**

## 3. Theoretical Framework: Legalization Without Institutionalization

Ma (2024) adapts the legalisation framework of Abbott, Keohane, Moravcsik, Slaughter and Snidal (2000) for the analysis of Chinese domestic policy. Ma reduces the original three dimensions of obligation, precision, and delegation into two operational dimensions of obligation and enforcement. Obligation measures the binding force of the policy on regulatory subjects, captured by indicators such as document issuer rank, target specificity, instrument specificity, and supplementary instruments. Enforcement measures the operational machinery for translating rules into decisions, captured by indicators such as inspection mechanisms, performance assessment, public disclosure, and coordination bodies. ## 5. Empirical Analysis

### 5.1 The National Baseline

The reform package consists of three central documents that any province must integrate. State Council General Office Notice 2025 No. 22, issued 22 June 2025, sets the macro framework. It specifies a ten-working-day overall timeline for credit repair processing, a three-working-day acceptance window, and a seven-working-day decision window. NDRC Order No. 36, issued 20 November 2025 and effective 1 April 2026, governs credit repair across all administrative domains. The order classifies misconduct as minor, general, or severe, with sectoral departments permitted to add categories of severe misconduct. Application conditions require correction of conduct, fulfilment of obligations, and a public credit pledge. The order establishes an administrative appeal channel through the Credit China website. SAMR Order No. 107, issued 21 November 2025 and effective 25 December 2025, governs credit repair within the market supervision domain only. The order uses the same trichotomy but quantifies monetary thresholds at 50,000 yuan for businesses and 5,000 yuan for individuals. Application conditions require active rectification, elimination of harm, and absence of recurrence in the same category. The order routes disputes to administrative review or court proceedings only.

The two ministerial orders differ in where substantive discretion is located. NDRC Order 36 delegates substantive discretion to sectoral departments by leaving phrases such as "corrected misconduct" and "other severe categories" open. SAMR Order 107 retains substantive discretion within the order itself through phrases such as "active rectification eliminating harm" and "same category violation." The two orders also differ in the accessibility of dispute resolution, with NDRC providing an administrative appeal channel and SAMR routing disputes to formal administrative review or court proceedings only.

### 5.2 Zhejiang Implementation

Zhejiang issued Provincial Government Decree No. 406 in March 2025, six months before the publication of NDRC Order No. 36. The decree institutionalises credit repair at provincial level with statutory force, fixing jurisdictional boundaries, intake procedures, and decision timelines. The accompanying Credit Repair One Thing implementation scheme builds a unified intake platform on the Zhelibàn digital service infrastructure.

At municipal level, Hangzhou has layered two further mechanisms. The Three Documents Together practice attaches a Credit Repair Notification and a Compliance Recommendation Letter to every Administrative Penalty Decision. The Execution Observation Period mechanism, formalised in April 2026, allows officials to designate a temporary buffer between violation determination and penalty enforcement activation.

Zhejiang GDP per capita is approximately 141,000 yuan in 2025, ranking among the top five provinces nationally. Private sector employment exceeds 80 percent of total non-agricultural employment. Export-oriented enterprises constitute a substantial fraction of provincial corporate activity.

(改写) **Prior empirical evidence from Zhejiang provides additional context on how political considerations enter the credit governance infrastructure.** Lin and Milhaupt (2023) analysed corporate social credit scores for 531 A-share listed companies in Zhejiang, the only province to have published such scores publicly. **They found that politically connected firms received systematically higher scores regardless of ownership type or state equity share. The result was driven by a "social responsibility" category (18.5 percent of the total score) that awards points for government-issued honors, charitable donations to Party-sanctioned causes, and volunteer service in Party-recognized activities. Neither better-governed nor more profitable firms scored higher. This finding indicates that Zhejiang's advanced credit governance infrastructure already embeds political considerations within scoring outcomes, which is directly relevant to the discretion question addressed later in this paper.**

### 5.3 Liaoning Implementation

Liaoning has not issued any provincial statute on credit repair as of mid-2026. The provincial response has run through two distinct tracks. The Credit Certificate Substitution reform, jointly implemented in September 2025 by the Provincial Development and Reform Commission, the Public Security Department, and the Provincial Data Administration, replaces no-violation certifications with specialised credit reports. The platform had issued approximately 1,300 specialised reports replacing roughly 68,000 certifications by late 2025. The reform sits adjacent to credit repair rather than overlapping with it.

For credit repair narrowly, Liaoning's institutional activity is concentrated in two centre cities. Dalian's Free Trade Zone pilot, launched in May 2021, established 24-hour completion for enterprise credit repair as a first-in-the-country initiative. Shenyang's Credit Repair One Thing reform, introduced in November 2024, instituted immediate municipal-level approval and a dedicated guidance line. The Dalian Municipal Social Credit Ordinance of July 2021 provides a municipal statutory anchor for credit governance in general but does not codify credit repair criteria.

Liaoning GDP per capita is approximately 80,000 yuan in 2025. State-owned enterprises retain a dominant position in the provincial economy. Private sector activity is concentrated in Dalian and Shenyang.

(改写) Figure 2 places the coded cases on the four-type matrix. Zhejiang's Decree No. 406 and SAMR Order 107 occupy Type 4 (strong obligation, strong enforcement). (改写) **Two features of this comparison require analytical attention. First, both provincial profiles leave substantive criteria for repair decisions ("corrected violation," "fulfilled obligations") undefined at the provincial level. This is true in Zhejiang despite Decree No. 406's extensive procedural codification. It is also true in Liaoning despite the absence of any provincial statute. Second, the municipal innovations layered on top of the national and provincial frameworks in both provinces either expand discretion (Hangzhou's Execution Observation Period adds a stage of official judgment) or compress processing time without touching substance (Dalian and Shenyang). Neither province's municipal experiments codify substantive criteria that the provincial or national framework left open. The functional outcome, that the official reviewing each application still decides who qualifies for repair, is comparable across both provinces despite the striking contrast in formal codification. ## 6. Discussion: Same Function, Different Vehicles

### 6.1 Hypothesis Assessment

(改写) The three hypotheses tested here collectively suggest a multi-factor explanation. No single hypothesis can be confirmed with the present evidence base, but each contributes to interpreting the observed pattern.

Provincial capacity (H1) appears to play an enabling role for procedural elaboration. The timing of Zhejiang Decree No. 406, published six months before the national NDRC Order No. 36, illustrates how capacity enables provincial policy innovation when provincial conditions generate substantive interest in advance of central directives. **However, the Sichuan case complicates a strong reading of H1. Sichuan's GDP per capita is closer to Liaoning's than to Zhejiang's, yet Sichuan's SCS document output places it in a higher tier than Liaoning. Fan and Chen (2024) provide a further twist. Their analysis of SCS pilot policy effects on entrepreneurship across 282 cities from 2012 to 2019 finds the promotive effect "more pronounced in regions characterized by weaker legal environments, lower levels of marketization, and poorer economic conditions." This finding runs opposite to a straight capacity-based reading of H1, since it suggests that credit institutions may substitute for, rather than reflect, provincial legal-institutional strength. H1 alone therefore cannot account for the full pattern of provincial variation.**

Private sector structure (H2) is consistent with the procedural divergence at the design level. Zhejiang's export-oriented private sector has structural reasons to demand predictable repair procedures, particularly for cross-jurisdictional operation and international trade finance. **Song et al. (2024) find that SCS improvement effects on corporate ESG performance are more pronounced in non-state-owned enterprises, which is consistent with the structural demand mechanism H2 proposes.** Direct confirmation for the credit repair domain requires firm-level decision data not currently available.

Prior enforcement intensity (H3) is consistent with the observed pattern if assumptions about Liaoning's retention of state-directed regulatory leverage during the active enforcement phase are correct. The Sichuan anomaly noted under H1 provides indirect support for H3 as an independent factor. Sichuan's higher SCS document output despite modest economic capacity suggests that political factors related to how provincial leadership treats credit governance operate independently of pure economic capacity. Direct confirmation of H3 requires comparative active-phase enforcement data not currently collected.

### 6.2 Same Function, Different Vehicles

The observed divergence between Zhejiang and Liaoning is not a deviation from the national framework but a working out of the variation space that the framework leaves open. The national reform package consisting of SC Notice 22, NDRC Order No. 36, and SAMR Order No. 107 represents what Ma (2024) describes as top institutional design. The framework establishes uniform timelines, classification standards, and platform requirements, but it leaves substantial space for provincial implementation choices along three dimensions.

First, substantive criteria for repair decisions are not exhaustively specified in any of the three national documents. Phrases such as "corrected misconduct," "fully fulfilled obligations," "active rectification," and "elimination of harm" leave room for sectoral and provincial interpretation. Second, the parallel existence of NDRC and SAMR tracks creates institutional pluralism that provinces must integrate. The two orders differ in substantive criteria, in dispute resolution channels, and in classification thresholds. Provincial implementation must decide how to combine the two tracks. Third, the role of municipal-level innovation is not constrained by the national framework. Cities can develop additional procedural mechanisms or processing innovations without conflicting with national directives.

The observed pattern is that provinces with the capacity, the structural demand, and the political-economic conditions to invest in elaborate provincial codification have done so. Provinces without those conditions have produced minimal provincial response on credit repair specifically and have allowed municipal experimentation within existing administrative discretion. The functional outcome at the level of repair decision content is comparable across the two provinces, even though the formal vehicles for preserving discretion differ.

### 6.3 Extension to Market Governance

第一段：Ma (2024) 的扩展方向。Ma 在户籍改革域识别了 legalization without institutionalization 模式。户籍改革涉及社会福利、人口移动、公民身份，属于面向公民的社会治理域。Credit repair 涉及企业市场准入、经济制裁、监管修复，属于面向企业的市场治理域。两个域的政策张力方向甚至相反：户籍改革中中央希望松地方希望收（保护财政），credit repair 中中央希望规范化（避免合法性危机）地方希望保留监管资本。如果同一 discretion preservation 机制在方向相反的政策张力中都出现，这说明该机制不来自任何一个域的特定政治经济结构，而来自更深层的治理逻辑。这一层论证是本文对 Ma 的真正扩展，不是简单的 "extends to another domain"。

第二段：接续 variegation 概念。Caprotti et al. (2024) 在 municipal SCS 层级识别了 variegation 作为 SCS 的结构性特征。本文在 provincial credit repair 层级观察到类似的 variegation 但机制不同。municipal 层的 variegation 反映不同城市对同一 national vision 的差异化实现。provincial 层的 variegation 反映不同省份对同一 national framework 的差异化 institutional response，且该 variegation 收敛于同一个 functional outcome，即实质判断标准的策略性留白。本文因此把 variegation 概念从描述性 (SCS 是不统一的) 推进到分析性 (variegation 是 discretion preservation 的 institutional expression)。

第三段：连接 Min Jiang (2020) 的历史谱系。Jiang 论证 SCS 延续帝国官员档案、danwei dang'an、道德档案立法未通过等历史序列。本文的经验发现——市场治理域出现 legalization without institutionalization——不是 2025 后突然出现的新现象，而是这条谱系在当前技术条件下的最新表现。本节点出这一点，让本文的具体经验贡献锚定到更长时段的治理连续性上，增加论证厚度。

结尾一句话：识别的具体 mechanism (strategic under-specification of substantive criteria within otherwise heavily codified frameworks) 值得在其他 recently codified domains 系统检验，作为进一步研究议题。

### 6.4 Robustness: The Intentionality Inference

【新写】本节 6.4 约 350 词。任务：处理 undefined criteria 论证的稳健性问题。Fable Claude 6/11 显式提醒过这个 gap，reviewer 会检查这一段。写作结构如下。

第一段：陈述两个 rival explanations。核心论断是"省级政府故意通过实质判断标准留白保留裁量"，但 undefined criteria 至少还可能反映两个替代解释。第一，drafting lag：省份还没时间去具体化，未来会补上。第二，regulatory deference：省份故意把具体化交给国家或部委层，等国家出台更具体的补充文件。

第二段：市级证据作为 robustness check。如果 undefined provincial criteria 反映 drafting lag 或 regulatory deference，操作层面上执行修复申请的城市应该要么自己补上具体化，要么显式向上等待。观察数据显示两种模式都不出现。Hangzhou 加执行观察期扩展了官员裁量而不是定义标准。Dalian 24 小时机制和 Shenyang 即时审批压缩处理时间而不改动实质标准。三个 administrative tier（国家、省、市）都在具体化实质标准这一维度留白，这一 pattern 让 drafting lag 假说站不住，因为 drafting lag 通常在具体操作层被补上而不是被复制。

第三段：连接 Tsai, Wang and Lin (2021) 的 fieldwork 证据。他们对 SCS 早期实施的观察显示，模糊性被地方官员作为战略性资源使用而非被动接受。本文观察到的三层留白模式与他们的 fieldwork 结论一致，进一步支持 deliberate preservation 而非 drafting lag 的解释。

### 6.5 Scope and Limitations

(改写) **This paper's contribution is limited to establishing the de jure baseline against which future de facto analysis can be conducted. Whether the discretion patterns identified in the codified texts also characterise actual repair decisions is a distinct empirical question. Decision-level data on approval rates, processing times in practice, and substantive criteria actually applied is not currently published by provincial platforms. If such data becomes available, the pattern of discretion preservation identified here could either be corroborated (comparable de facto discretion in both provinces despite different de jure vehicles) or complicated (systematically different de facto discretion despite the de jure similarity in leaving criteria undefined).**

The case selection covers two provinces and a small number of municipalities within each. Future expansion to additional provinces with different combinations of capacity, private sector concentration, and prior enforcement intensity would test whether the observed pattern generalises. Plausible additional cases include Guangdong, Sichuan, Jiangsu, and Heilongjiang, each of which represents a different combination of the structural conditions hypothesised to shape implementation. The reform package took effect between December 2025 and April 2026, so the window for institutional observation is short and provincial responses may continue to evolve. A longitudinal follow-up at six-month and twelve-month intervals would provide stronger evidence of stable institutional patterns.

## 7. Conclusion

【新写】Conclusion 约 400 词，三段结构。

第一段：重述核心 finding。要点：本文证明在 2025-2026 credit repair 国家统一框架下，Zhejiang 和 Liaoning 产出了截然不同的省级 institutional response。Zhejiang 在国家框架生效前六个月出台了具有法定效力的省级立法并建立统一数字平台。Liaoning 至今没有省级 credit repair 立法，其制度活动流向 adjacent 领域。尽管这一对比鲜明，两省都在实质判断标准这一关键位置留白，市级创新都不填补这一留白。

第二段：陈述 broader implication。要点：这一比较支持把 2025-2026 改革解读为 Ma (2024) 意义上的 legalization without institutionalization。formal legal codification 并没有对行政裁量产生 binding constraint，因为裁量是通过 substantive criteria 的策略性留白保留，而不是通过 formalization 的缺失或存在保留。这一 finding 把 Ma 的户籍改革结论扩展到市场治理，并识别了具体的 institutional mechanism——在成文化框架内对实质判断标准的策略性留白。这个机制在两个方向相反的政策张力域都运作，说明它是中国治理的通用特征而非任何单一政策域的产物。

第三段：回扣开篇的合法性危机。要点：credit repair 改革本身就是对 2020-2024 惩戒阶段合法性危机的回应。formal path 已经建立，但那条 path 上的每个决定仍由省级和市级官员的裁量决定。这一制度安排究竟产生了改革意图建立的 corrective 功能，还是把 discretion 问题以 codified form 保留下来，是未来几年 de facto 运作会回答的问题。这个 open ending 保护论证不越出证据边界，也 signal 你意识到 de jure/de facto 张力是下一步研究议题。
